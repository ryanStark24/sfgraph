# sfgraph audit, October 2026

This is the Task 13 audit sweep of the readiness plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-09-sfgraph-readiness.md`.

The method had three stages:
1. **Find:** six read-only finder agents each covered one area: core correctness, dangling edges, runtime perf, reliability, security, and MCP tools (correctness and UX).
2. **Verify:** a separate agent independently reproduced or rejected every finding.
3. **Act on confirmed findings:** each confirmed Critical, High or Medium finding gets a failing regression test written by an agent other than the fixer, then a fix. Mediums that are not fixed and all Lows get a ruling.

Totals: 99 findings, of which 90 were CONFIRMED, 5 were DUPLICATE and 4 were REJECTED. The confirmed findings break down as 8 Critical, 16 High, 32 Medium and 34 Low.

Raw finder and verifier files (JSON) are kept in the SDD workspace and are not committed.
